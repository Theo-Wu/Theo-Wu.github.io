## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at [The University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/). I am currently supervised by [Dr. Zahra Montazeri](https://personalpages.manchester.ac.uk/staff/zahra.montazeri/), co-supervised by [Prof. Steve Pettifer](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=yUwh2bEAAAAJ&hl=en). I received my M.Sc. at [University College London (UCL)](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/) tutored by [Prof. Tobias Ritschel](https://www.homepages.ucl.ac.uk/~ucactri/) and completed my B.Eng. at [Sun Yat-sen University](https://www.sysu.edu.cn/sysuen/) tutored by [Prof. Kai Huang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=70_wl8kAAAAJ&hl=en).

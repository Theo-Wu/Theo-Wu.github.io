## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at [The University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/). I am currently supervised by [Dr. Zahra Montazeri](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/persons/zahra.montazeri), co-supervised by [Prof. Steve Pettifer](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/persons/steve.pettifer). I received my M.Sc. at [University College London (UCL)](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/) and completed my B.Eng. at [Sun Yat-sen University](https://www.sysu.edu.cn/sysuen/).
